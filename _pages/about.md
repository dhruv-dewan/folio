Hi! I’m Dhruv, an undergraduate student studying Computer Science and Mathematics at the University of Maryland, College Park. I’m passionate about the intersection of artificial intelligence, systems engineering, and financial technology.

Currently, I am an AI Architecture Intern at Guardian Life, where I am working on building scalable systems for multi-model orchestration and integrating LLMs into real-world infrastructure. Previously, I interned as a Machine Learning Engineer at a startup, where I built hybrid search systems and automated MLOps pipelines.

Outside of work, I’m actively exploring the capabilities and behavior of large language models, and working on a side project focused on AI-powered market insights for retail investors. At UMD, I’m part of the honors program, where I’ve led an applied ML research project on skin cancer detection.

I’m driven by a desire to build impactful AI systems and eventually want to be part of fast-moving environments where research, product, and infrastructure converge, whether that’s at the frontier of industry or something I build myself.
